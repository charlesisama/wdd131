💯 porem Information has become one of the most valuable resources in modern society. Governments, organizations, and individuals depend on information for decision-making, planning, service delivery, and innovation. However, the value of information can only be fully realized when it is properly managed, protected, accessed, and used responsibly. This is where information policy becomes important. Information policy refers to the set of rules, laws, principles, and guidelines that govern how information is created, collected, stored, shared, protected, and used. In Nigeria, the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA) plays a major role in regulating and developing the country’s information technology ecosystem.
