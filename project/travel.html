Travel Advisory

This page provides general guidance for visitors traveling within Nigeria. Conditions can vary by location and time, so always check official sources for current updates.

Before You Travel

Documents Confirm visa requirements, passport validity (6+ months recommended), and keep digital and printed copies of key IDs.
Health Review vaccination guidance and carry essentials such as insect repellent and basic medications.
Money Plan for both card and cash options. Keep smaller denominations for daily transport and local vendors.
Connectivity Consider a local SIM or eSIM for reliable data access during your stay.

On-the-Ground Safety Tips

  • Use reputable ride-hailing services where available and confirm vehicle details before entering.
  • Avoid displaying valuables in crowded areas.
  • Prefer daytime travel for unfamiliar routes.
  • Ask trusted locals or hotel staff about areas to avoid at night.

Tip: Before heading out, save your accommodation location offline (Google Maps) and share your live location with a trusted contact—especially for evening outings or unfamiliar routes.

Culture & Etiquette

Greetings Matter Simple greetings create a positive first impression.
Ask Before Photos Always request permission before photographing people or sacred sites.
Dress Appropriately Some areas are conservative—dress according to context.
Respect Local Customs Be mindful of religious practices and cultural traditions.

Emergency Preparedness

Keep emergency contacts saved on your phone, know the address of your accommodation, and maintain awareness of your surroundings. If you feel unsafe, move to a well-lit public area and contact assistance.
