Site Name

Explore Nigeria

Site Purpose

Expore Nigeria is a Cultural and travel information website that helps visitors discover Nigeria's major cities, landmark, and Culturalhighlights.
The site will present featured destinations, quick facts and engaging content using Responsive design and javascript driven interactions.

Target Audience

  • Tourist and first-time visitors planning a trip to Nigeria
  • Nigerians in the diaspora who wants a cultural reference site
  • Students and curious learners who want a simple overview of Nigeria
